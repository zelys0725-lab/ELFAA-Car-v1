Platform Overview

What is ELFAA Car Rental?

ELFAA is a full-stack, AI-assisted self-drive car rental management platform built for the Philippines market. It handles everything from public browsing and booking, to automated document verification, to real-time GPS fleet monitoring — all in one system.

🚗

Self-Drive Fleet

Browse and book from a curated fleet of Sedans, SUVs, Vans, Pickups, and Sports cars with transparent daily rates, seat capacity, and fuel type information.

🤖

AI-Powered ID Verification

Uploaded government-issued IDs are scanned locally by Tesseract OCR, which reads all text, extracts key identity fields, and cross-references them against your registered profile automatically.

📡

Live GPS Tracking

Every vehicle can be tracked in real-time using a smartphone as the GPS transmitter — no external hardware needed. The admin fleet map updates every 8 seconds with live coordinates and speed.

💬

Smart AI Chatbot

An intent-driven virtual assistant available 24/7 to answer questions about pricing, booking requirements, promos, fleet availability, and contact information — powered by a local rule engine, no external API needed.

📊

Admin Analytics

Comprehensive dashboards for earnings, bookings, vehicle performance, profit analytics, operational tasks, daily schedules, billing records, and staff management.

🔔

Multi-Channel Notifications

Configurable booking confirmation messages sent via Email (SMTP), SMS (Semaphore), or Viber to keep renters updated at every booking status change.

ℹ️
Technology StackBuilt with Laravel 12, Inertia.js, React, and Vite. AI verification uses Tesseract OCR (open-source, local, no cloud needed). GPS mapping uses Leaflet + OpenStreetMap (free, no API key). GPS coordinates are captured via the browser's Web Geolocation API on a mounted smartphone.
Account & Registration

Creating Your Account

Every customer must have a verified account before placing a booking. Registration is free and takes under 2 minutes.

Registration Steps

  • Go to the homepage and click Register / Sign Up
  • Enter your full legal name, valid email address, and phone number
  • Choose a strong password (minimum 8 characters)
  • Your customer account is created instantly
  • Add your Driver's License Number, ID type, and ID number in Profile Settings
  • Upload your required identity documents (see table on the right)

  • Once verified, you can place fully confirmed bookings

Booking Process

How to Book a Vehicle

The booking flow is designed to be quick, transparent, and automated. Here is every step from browsing the fleet to completing your rental.

1

Browse the Fleet

Log in and go to Browse Fleet. Filter by vehicle type (Sedan, SUV, Van, Pickup, Sports) and seating capacity. Each card shows the daily rate, transmission, fuel type, features, and the meeting/depot location.

2

Check Real-Time Availability

Use the Availability Calendar on each vehicle card. Booked dates are shown in red, available dates in green. The system blocks double-booking automatically in real time.

3

Fill Booking Details

Select pick-up and return dates/times. Enter pick-up and drop-off locations (pre-filled with the main garage or enter a custom address). Optionally apply a promo code. Choose payment method: Cash on Delivery or Online Payment. Select any add-on services.

4

Booking Submitted — Pending Review

Your booking is saved with status Pending. Staff reviews your documents and booking details. You receive a notification (Email/SMS/Viber) once the status is updated.

5

Confirmation & Active Rental

Once approved: Confirmed. On pick-up day the booking becomes Active. GPS tracking activates if configured for the vehicle. At the end: Completed.

6

Rate & Review

After completion, submit a star rating and written comment from your booking history. Ratings are visible to admin for fleet quality monitoring.

💳

Payment Methods

  • Cash on Delivery — Pay in full upon vehicle pick-up at the depot
  • Online Payment — Bank transfer or e-wallet (coordinate with admin after booking)
  • Promo codes apply automatic discounts at checkout (percentage or fixed PHP amount)
  • Only one promo code per booking is allowed
📦

Add-On Services

  • Add-ons (e.g., GPS Device, Child Seat, Extra Driver Insurance) can be selected during booking
  • Add-ons are priced per day and automatically included in your total cost breakdown
  • Only add-ons with Available status can be selected
  • A detailed price breakdown modal shows your full cost before confirmation
AI & Machine Learning

How AI-Powered ID Verification Works

ELFAA uses a multi-layer automated document analysis pipeline powered by Tesseract OCR (Google's open-source, locally-run optical character recognition engine) combined with custom rules-based AI to verify the authenticity and accuracy of your uploaded identity documents — completely offline, no external cloud API required.

1

Document Upload & Secure Storage

You upload a photo (JPEG/PNG) or PDF of your ID. The file is stored on the server's private disk — never on a public URL. All document access requires authentication and is protected by Laravel Gate authorization policies.

Private Disk Storage · Laravel Gate
2

Tesseract OCR Text Extraction

The server runs Tesseract OCR locally against your document image. Tesseract reads every character on the ID and returns two things: the raw extracted text (all words it could read), and an authentic per-word confidence score averaged into a single mean confidence percentage (0–100%).

Tesseract OCR Engine · Local · No Cloud
# Example raw OCR output from a Philippine Driver's License image LAND TRANSPORTATION OFFICE REPUBLIC OF THE PHILIPPINES LICENSE NO: N01-21-012345 NAME: REYES, MARIA SANTOS DATE OF BIRTH: 15 JAN 1995 EXPIRATION DATE: 2026-01-15 BLOOD TYPE: O+ SEX: F → Mean Confidence Score: 87.4% | Words Recognized: 24
3

Document Type Auto-Detection

The AI scans for specific keywords in the extracted text to automatically classify what type of document was uploaded. It recognizes: Philippine Driver's License, Passport, PhilSys National ID, UMID/SSS ID, Postal ID, and Proof of Billing documents (Meralco, Maynilad, PLDT/Globe bills, etc.).

Keyword Classification Engine
4

Structured Identity Field Extraction

Custom regex pattern matching and heuristic text analysis extract specific fields from the raw OCR text:

  • Full Name — via keyword anchors (NAME:, BEARER:) or scanning for lines with 2–5 capitalized words, filtering out known non-name keywords (REPUBLIC, TRANSPORT, etc.)
  • ID / License Number — PH Driver's License format (N01-21-012345), PhilSys 16-digit, Passport alphanumeric (P1234567)
  • Date of Birth — Multiple format patterns: ISO, MM/DD/YYYY, DD MMM YYYY
  • Expiration Date — Keyword-anchored patterns (EXPIRY, VALID UNTIL, EXP. DATE) plus fallback scan for future-year dates in 2020–2040 range
Regex + Heuristic Parsing
5

Cross-Reference Against Your Customer Profile

Every extracted field is compared against the data you registered in your customer account. Each comparison generates a pass / warning / fail result that feeds the overall verification score:

Image Readability (OCR Confidence)87%

Rules-Based Scoring Engine
6

AI Recommendation Generation

Based on all checks combined, the system automatically generates one of four recommendations displayed to the admin:

Approve Ready Manual Review Likely Reject OCR Failed – Manual Review
Decision Recommendation Engine
7

Human Admin Review & Final Decision

An admin reviews the full verification panel: the actual document image (with zoom, rotate, and full-screen controls), all extracted OCR fields vs. your profile data, the confidence score, the AI recommendation, the raw OCR text, and the complete timestamped audit trail. The admin makes the final legally-binding decision: Approve, Reject (with reason), or Request Resubmission (with instructions).

Human-in-the-Loop Final Decision

Privacy & SecurityYour document files are stored on the private server disk, never exposed publicly. Only authenticated admin/staff can view your documents — and only after passing Laravel Gate authorization checks. Every document access, approval, rejection, or reprocessing event is recorded in an immutable audit log with timestamp, actor name, role, and action type.

Live GPS Fleet Tracking

How GPS Tracking Works

ELFAA uses a zero-hardware-cost GPS system where a smartphone acts as the in-car GPS transmitter. No physical GPS device or paid mapping API is needed — just a phone mounted in the vehicle running our web transmitter page.

📱
Smartphone

Browser Geolocation API reads GPS coordinates at high accuracy using device sensors

📡
Ping API

Phone sends lat/lng + speed + heading every 3–30 seconds to /api/gps/ping

🗄️
Laravel Backend

Updates vehicle's current position and appends a breadcrumb to the GPS history log table

🗺️
Admin Fleet Map

Leaflet + OpenStreetMap renders live vehicle pins, speed, status, and 24h route history

Phone Transmitter Features

  • High-accuracy GPS via navigator.geolocation.watchPosition() with enableHighAccuracy: true
  • Live digital speedometer display in km/h
  • Latitude / longitude shown to 6 decimal places
  • GPS accuracy radius indicator in meters
  • Configurable transmission interval: 3, 5, 10, or 30 seconds
  • Screen Wake Lock API — prevents the phone screen from sleeping while driving, ensuring continuous GPS uptime
  • Real-time activity log showing the timestamp and result of each ping
  • Works in any modern mobile browser (Chrome for Android, Safari for iOS)
  • Also supports the open-source Traccar Client Android/iOS app via standard Traccar HTTP protocol

Admin Fleet Monitor Features

  • Free OpenStreetMap tiles — no Google Maps API key or paid plan required
  • Auto-refreshes all vehicle positions every 8 seconds
  • Color-coded status markers: Moving Idle Offline
  • "Focus Pin" button centers the map on any selected vehicle
  • Route History — draws the last 24 hours of GPS breadcrumbs as a polyline path on the map
  • One-click copy of the smartphone transmitter URL for any vehicle
  • Vehicle cards show speed, coordinates, last ping time, and active renter name

GPS Vehicle Status Logic

StatusConditionVisual
MovingLast ping was less than 2 minutes ago AND current speed is greater than 3 km/hAnimated pulsing green dot on map
Idle / ParkedLast ping < 2 min ago AND speed ≤ 3 km/h, OR last ping was 2–10 minutes agoStatic amber dot on map
OfflineNo ping received for more than 10 minutes, or GPS was never activated for this vehicleRed dot, "Signal Lost" label
🎓
For Thesis/Research UseThis GPS telemetry system was engineered specifically for academic thesis-level research into IoT fleet telematics using consumer mobile hardware. All source data (latitude, longitude, speed, heading, timestamp) is persisted in the vehicle_gps_logs database table and can be exported for data analysis. The system also supports the free Traccar Client app as an alternative transmitter using /api/gps/traccar?id=DEVICE&lat=...&lon=...

AI Virtual Assistant

ELFAA AI Chatbot

The floating chat bubble (bottom-right of the website) is a local intent-classification AI assistant that answers customer questions using live database data — no external AI API or internet connection required for the AI logic itself.

How the AI Intent Engine Works

  • Converts user input to lowercase and runs it through regex keyword pattern matching to identify the user's intent
  • Each intent maps to a dynamic response function that queries live data (real fleet list, real prices, real active promos from the database)
  • Generates contextual quick-reply chips for the most likely follow-up questions
  • Understands Filipino language inputs: magkano, musta, kamusta, kamano, book, etc.
  • Gracefully falls back to a human contact/escalation message for unrecognized queries
  • No personal data is ever sent to a third-party AI service

Rules & Policies

Terms, Rules & Rental Policies

All users must comply with these platform rules. Violations may result in booking cancellation, account suspension, or legal action.

✅

Customer Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate, truthful personal information at registration
  • Upload clear, authentic, and unaltered identity documents
  • Ensure your Driver's License is valid and non-expired at time of rental
  • Return the vehicle in the same condition as when picked up
  • Report any accidents, damage, or incidents immediately to ELFAA staff
  • Respect the rental return date and time agreed in your booking
  • Do not sub-rent or lend the vehicle to unauthorized third parties
  • Follow all Philippine traffic laws and road safety regulations
🚫

Prohibited Actions

  • Uploading fake, edited, or fraudulently altered ID documents
  • Impersonating another person during account registration or document upload
  • Using the vehicle for any illegal activities
  • Operating the vehicle outside agreed areas without admin permission
  • Disabling, removing, or tampering with the GPS tracking phone/device
  • Allowing unlicensed drivers to operate the vehicle
  • Smoking inside the vehicle
  • Transporting hazardous, flammable, or illegal materials
🔒

Document Privacy Policy

  • All uploaded documents are stored on the private server disk — never on a public URL
  • Only authenticated admin/staff with verified system access can view your documents
  • Every single document access and decision is recorded in an immutable audit trail
  • Uploading fraudulent documents is a criminal offense (RA 10175, Cybercrime Prevention Act)
  • Rejected documents can be corrected and resubmitted at any time
  • Document data is used solely for identity verification — never shared with third parties
🔄

Cancellation & Late Return

  • Cancellations must be requested through your customer dashboard booking history
  • Cancellation fees are at admin discretion based on how close to the rental date
  • Late returns may incur additional charges per hour or per day at the agreed daily rate
  • Contact admin immediately if you cannot return the vehicle on time
  • Failure to return the vehicle without contact may be treated as unauthorized use

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions Answered

Quick, accurate answers to the most common questions about the platform, AI systems, and booking process.

How long does AI ID verification take?+

The Tesseract OCR scan runs automatically within seconds of your document upload. However, the final human admin approval typically takes 1–24 hours depending on submission time. You will receive a notification (Email/SMS/Viber, if configured) once your document is reviewed.

Why did my OCR result show a low confidence score?+

Low OCR confidence (below 70%) typically means the uploaded image was blurry, too dark, too small, had heavy shadows over the text, or was a heavily compressed JPEG. Try re-uploading a clearer photo taken in daylight. After re-uploading, you can also request OCR reprocessing from the document panel in your dashboard. The admin can also manually trigger reprocessing.

Can I book without a Driver's License?+

No. A valid Philippine Driver's License is mandatory as the primary government ID for all vehicle rentals. This is both a legal requirement and an insurance condition. The license must be non-expired on the scheduled rental date. Expired licenses will be flagged by the AI and rejected by admin.

Is the GPS tracking visible to me as a customer?+

The live GPS fleet map is accessible only to admin and staff accounts. As a customer, you will not see a real-time tracking map of yourself. GPS tracking is used exclusively for fleet safety, vehicle recovery, and operational management purposes by ELFAA staff.

How do promo codes work?+

Enter a promo code in the designated field during booking. The system automatically validates the code against the database of active promos and applies the discount to your total. Discounts can be either a fixed peso amount (e.g., PHP 500 off) or a percentage (e.g., 10% off). Only one promo code is allowed per booking.

What technology powers the AI chatbot — does it send my data to ChatGPT?+

No. The ELFAA chatbot uses a completely local, custom-built intent engine based on keyword regex pattern matching — no external AI API (ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.) is called. All responses come from live database queries on your own server. No user messages or personal data are ever sent to third-party AI services. The chatbot also understands common Filipino language inputs.

What happens if I cancel a confirmed booking?+

Cancellation requests are submitted through your dashboard booking history and reviewed by admin. Cancellation fees may apply depending on how close to the rental date the request is made. For urgent cancellations, contact admin directly via the chatbot or the provided contact details. Cancellation processing is at admin discretion.

How do I use my phone as a GPS tracker inside the car?+

Admin will provide you (or mount the phone themselves) with a unique tracker URL in the format /tracker/[KEY]. Open that URL in Chrome or Safari on the smartphone, tap "Start GPS Transmission," allow location access when prompted, and mount the phone on the car's dashboard holder. The phone will send live GPS coordinates to the admin map every few seconds. Make sure the phone stays connected to mobile data or Wi-Fi for uninterrupted transmission.
