Architecture

How Multi-Factor Authentication Works in ELFAA

Multi-Factor Authentication enhances account security by requiring two distinct identity proofs: something you know (password) and something you have (rolling 6-digit Authenticator code or Email OTP).

📱
Primary

Authenticator App (TOTP)

Standard RFC 6238 time-based tokens. Generates rolling 6-digit codes every 30 seconds on Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password. Works 100% offline without cell reception.

✉️
Zero-Setup

Email One-Time Password

Dispatches a secure 6-digit PIN to the user's verified email via the configured SMTP server. Protected with single-use invalidation, cooldown timers, and expiration windows.

🔑
Recovery

Backup Recovery Codes

A set of 8 single-use alphanumeric emergency codes (e.g. 8F3K-92A1). Allows instant account recovery if a user loses access to their phone or email inbox.

User Setup

2. Connecting an Authenticator App (Google / Microsoft / Authy)

Step-by-step instructions for any user (Customer, Staff, or Admin) to connect their mobile authenticator app.

1

Access Security & 2FA Settings

Click your name in the top right header menu → Select Security & Profile (2FA) (or navigate to /profile).

2

Click "Set Up Authenticator"

On the Two-Factor Authentication card, locate the Authenticator App panel and click Set Up Authenticator. A modal will open displaying a unique QR code and secret key.

3

Scan the QR Code

Open your preferred Authenticator app on your smartphone:

  • Google Authenticator: Tap the + button in the bottom right → Scan a QR code.
  • Microsoft Authenticator: Tap + (Add account) → Other account → Scan QR code.
  • Apple iOS Passwords: Settings → Passwords → elfaa.ph → Set Up Verification Code.
💡
Can't scan the QR code? Click the copy icon next to the manual secret key in the modal, open your authenticator app, choose Enter key manually, paste the key, and set Type to Time-based.
4

Verify & Activate

Enter the current 6-digit rolling code displayed in your app into Step 2 of the modal, then click Verify & Activate. Your Authenticator 2FA is now active, and emergency backup codes will be generated automatically!

Zero-Setup Method

3. Enabling Email One-Time Password (OTP)

Email OTP provides a zero-friction second factor that requires no smartphone apps. Codes are sent directly to the user's verified account email.

1

Ensure SMTP Email Service is Active (Admins)

Before using Email OTP, verify that SMTP settings are configured under Admin → Settings → SMTP Email. You can use Gmail SMTP, SendGrid, Mailgun, Amazon SES, or custom SMTP servers.

2

Click "Enable Email OTP" in Profile

In Security & Profile (2FA), click Enable Email OTP on the Email One-Time Password card. A 6-digit test code will be immediately dispatched to your registered email.

3

Confirm Test Code

Check your email inbox for the subject "Your ELFAA Verification Code: [XXXXXX]", enter the 6-digit PIN in the confirmation modal, and click Verify & Enable.

Account Recovery

4. Managing Emergency Backup Recovery Codes

Backup codes are single-use recovery keys that let you log in if you lose your phone, break your device, or cannot access your email.

🔑
Crucial Step

How Backup Codes Protect Your Account

Each user gets 8 single-use codes. When you log in with a backup code, it is immediately invalidated and cannot be used again.

⚠️
Never store backup codes on the same phone as your Authenticator app. Save them in a secure password manager (1Password, Bitwarden, Apple Keychain) or print/download them as a text file.
1

How to Generate or Regenerate Codes

Go to Security & Profile (2FA) → Click Manage Recovery Codes → Enter your account password to generate a fresh set. Note: Generating new codes permanently invalidates all previous ones.

2

How to Log In with a Backup Code

On the /mfa/challenge login screen, click "Lost your phone or email? Use a backup recovery code" → Enter any unused 8-character code → Click Verify & Proceed.

Administrator Override

5. Emergency Administrative MFA Reset

What happens if a customer or staff member completely loses their phone, email, and backup codes? Administrators can perform an emergency recovery reset.

🚨
Admin Only

How to Reset MFA for a Locked User

1

Open Admin Users Management

In the Admin Dashboard, click the Users tab in the left sidebar navigation.

2

Locate the User & Check 2FA Status

Inspect the 2FA / MFA column. Users with active protection display App 2FA or Email OTP.

3

Click "🛡️ Reset MFA"

Click the 🛡️ Reset MFA button next to the user. A confirmation modal will appear. Confirm the prompt to immediately wipe their MFA secret, deactivate 2FA, and purge old backup codes.

4

User Can Now Log In Directly

The user can immediately log in using their primary password, and can re-enroll in 2FA from their profile settings.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

Quick solutions for common 2FA issues encountered by users and administrators.

Clock Drift / Code Rejection Error

TOTP relies on accurate device time. If your authenticator app code is rejected, check that your smartphone's time is set to "Set Automatically" (Network Time) in your phone settings. Even a 60-second time drift can cause code verification to fail.

Email OTP Not Arriving

1. Check your spam/junk folder for emails from ELFAA Car Rental.
2. Admins should verify SMTP credentials under Admin → Settings → SMTP Email and send a test email to ensure the mailer is functioning.

Locked Out from Too Many Attempts

If you fail verification 5 times consecutively, the session is terminated for brute-force defense. Return to the login screen and sign in again with your email and password to receive a fresh verification challenge.

Switching Between Email & App During Login

On the /mfa/challenge screen, you can toggle between Email OTP, App Code, and Backup Code using the top pill buttons at any time during login.
